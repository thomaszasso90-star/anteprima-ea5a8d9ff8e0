About us

Da Silvio is a restaurant, pizzeria and bar in Agordo, a short walk from the village hotels, with plenty of parking right outside.

From the kitchen: thin wood-fired pizza, fresh pasta and local mountain dishes, from venison to mushrooms and polenta, in generous portions. Service is quick, we speak English, and hikers coming off the Alta Via 1 always find a warm table.
